SOLO CAMPAIGN · STARTER GUIDE

Begin your Victorum campaign

A first-session roadmap for learning the campaign's rhythm without trying to memorise the whole rulebook at once.

Rules authority: Treat the current official Victorum rulebook, card/component text, and official FAQs/errata as definitive. This is a learning roadmap, not a replacement for the rules.

What you're trying to do

Victorum is a solo-only campaign. You lead one of eight heroes, assemble and develop a squad, travel through a four-act campaign, defeat Primuses and ultimately confront Pluto's Scions. Your choices across the campaign affect the tools and forces available to you later.

First session checklist

  1. Gather the Victorum components, rulebook, reference sheet and hero tracker.
  2. Read the rulebook's setup and campaign structure sections before resolving your first event.
  3. Choose a hero and record the starting information exactly as directed by the rules.
  4. Set up the current campaign act and week, including the correct event and faction stacks.
  5. Before the first battle, check the specific arena and opponent instructions; component text can create exceptions to general rules.
  6. Track squad, health, resources and campaign changes as they happen rather than relying on memory.

A useful decision loop

  1. Campaign: What is the objective and what choices are available this week?
  2. Preparation: Which squad roles and skills answer the upcoming challenge?
  3. Battle plan: Identify the immediate threat, key objective and vulnerable units before moving.
  4. Resolve carefully: Check targeting priorities, skill timing and any arena-specific rules.
  5. After action: Apply all rewards, losses, healing and progression before continuing.

What to learn first

  • Basic unit movement, attacks and skill keywords.
  • How arena objectives change the meaning of a “good” attack.
  • How your hero and squad complement one another.
  • How campaign choices trade immediate safety against future strength.

Strategy notebook

After each battle, record the scenario, hero, squad, major threat, key decision, outcome and one lesson. Over time, these notes can support matchup guides and hero-specific recommendations.
