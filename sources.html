How conflicts are resolved

  1. Follow the current official rulebook and official FAQ/errata.
  2. Check the relevant card, chip, arena or scenario text for an explicit exception.
  3. Use official integration rules for cross-game content.
  4. Use community discussions only as leads for questions, not as authority over official rules.
  5. If unresolved, label the issue “Needs verification” and avoid presenting a guess as a definitive ruling.
