PROJECT ROADMAP

Full-collection research plan

Build an accurate, navigable solo reference in useful stages, with clear links from every guide back to source material.

Research workstreams

Rules foundation

  • Victorum rulebook: setup, campaign structure, weekly choices, battle flow, progression and ending.
  • Remastered rulebook: general rules, troop/controller model, turn structure and five modes.
  • Unit Skills Reference Sheet: establish consistent definitions and cross-game differences.
  • Official FAQ and errata: capture question, ruling, affected component and date/version where available.

Victorum

  • Eight heroes: strengths, trade-offs and beginner recommendations.
  • Campaign route planning, events, opportunities, contests and battles.
  • Squad composition, recruitment, upgrades and unit preservation.
  • Primus and Scion threat profiles, arena objectives and matchup plans.
  • Expansion modules, alternate campaign options and save/continuation checklist.

Remastered

  • All five modes: setup, players, AI, objectives, resources and win conditions.
  • Solo AI turn flow and targeting priority examples.
  • Factions, heroes, units, tactics, Titans and Immortals.
  • Scenario difficulty, repeatable challenges and learning progression.

Expansion and compatibility inventory

  • Victorum base game and Remastered base game.
  • Pandora's Ruin: identify each module and its effect on Victorum play.
  • Pandora's Wake: identify content and rules for Remastered.
  • Pandora's Might: document its cross-game role and rules.
  • The Forge micro-expansion: verify components and exact integration instructions.
  • Mercury's Boots and associated rules/reference materials.
  • All official integration rules and any newer products or downloadable updates found in the publisher's current catalogue.

Evidence labels

  • Official rule: supported by current official rulebook or component text.
  • Official clarification: supported by publisher FAQ/errata.
  • Strategy: reasoned recommendation, not a mandatory rule.
  • Community variant: clearly labelled as unofficial and optional.
  • Needs verification: unresolved; do not rely on it as a ruling.

Release checklist

  • Every page has working navigation and a route back to the home page.
  • Mobile layout and readable light theme checked.
  • Every rules claim links to a relevant official source where possible.
  • Compatibility claims checked against current integration rules.
  • GitHub Pages build and published URL verified before declaring deployment successful.
